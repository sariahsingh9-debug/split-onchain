TRUST LAYER

KNOW WHAT
YOU APPROVE

SPLIT is designed around explicit wallet approval, exact payment verification, transparent creator-revenue routing and visible third-party dependencies.

SELF-CUSTODY

Your wallet stays yours

SPLIT never asks for a seed phrase or private key. Blockchain actions are approved inside the connected wallet.

PAYMENT VERIFICATION

Submitted is not Paid

A transaction is marked Paid only after SPLIT verifies the on-chain payer, recipient, asset, exact amount and successful confirmation.

EMAIL DELIVERY

Delivery has a real status

Participant emails are sent through Resend. Accepted, delivered, delayed and bounced states can be stored through signed Resend webhooks.

PERSISTENCE

Records live server-side

Private SPLIT records, payment states, creator indexes and token directory records are stored server-side in private Render Key Value storage rather than relying on one browser.
